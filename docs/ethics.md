# Ethics, disclosure, and data handling

## Historical study disclosure

The author states that the historical study did **not** obtain site-owner permission or institutional ethical approval, did **not** send disclosure notifications, and did **not** conduct manual review. These are author-reported facts, not conclusions inferred from missing logs.

The study's findings are automated assertions. They are not manually verified exploitable vulnerabilities or verified working credentials. This disclosure does not retroactively create permission or ethical approval, and does not resolve a journal's requirements.

## Traffic and settings

The scanner retrieves resources and performs additional path, synthetic-origin, TLS, DNS, and registry operations. It is read-oriented, not traffic-free. A publicly reachable site and a content blocklist do not establish consent or lack of impact.

Optional live-key verification exists in source and defaults off in the inspected path. The exact historical deployed configuration remains unverified; the absence of manual review is not proof that an automated verification option was off. The inspected shared HTTP session identifies itself as `Bravo6-Scanner/8.5`, but the historical User-Agent is not established by a deployment record.

## What the documentation publishes

This site uses aggregate results, source descriptions, and synthetic API examples. It does not intentionally embed raw scan reports, extracted credentials, lists of vulnerable targets, or live deployment addresses. Existing site-level records elsewhere in Git are a separate publication-review issue, not automatically cleared by this site's aggregate presentation.

## Preserve originals, review derivatives

Do not redact original experimental files in place. Preserve the evidence snapshot and prepare a separate sanitized derivative with its own provenance. Review target identifiers, evidence strings, URLs, timestamps, small groups, and links that could reconnect records to sites. Domain hashes alone do not guarantee anonymity.

The local paper/reanalysis package is not automatically a public dataset. A reproducibility statement must identify exactly which materials are accessible and which remain unavailable.

## Integrity is not authorization

SHA-256 checks verify that files match a recorded snapshot. They do not prove consent, an ethical review, original acquisition time, or a complete chain of custody. Ethical statements and technical integrity checks have different purposes.

## Future use

Establish the appropriate authorization and institutional review before future collection, define the operational scope and data-retention policy, and plan disclosure where warranted. This documentation rewrite performed no new target scan, key verification, or infrastructure deployment.
